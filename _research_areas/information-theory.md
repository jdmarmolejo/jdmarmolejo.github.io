Cells make decisions to maximise survival, conserve resources, and respond appropriately to external stimuli. My research in biological information theory focuses on information flow in gene regulatory networks. I use the chemical master equation (CME) and the linear‑noise approximation (LNA) to characterise how cells transmit, store, and process information about their environment despite the intrinsic stochasticity of gene expression.

## Open questions

- How much information can a single gene encode about a time-varying signal?
- What architectural features of regulatory networks maximize information
  throughput per molecule?
